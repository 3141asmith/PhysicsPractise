# GCSE question bank: specification mapping

Checked against the AQA subject-content pages on 21 September 2026. These are original exercises, not copied examination questions. `spec` values refer to the separate Physics specification (4.x); each question links to the relevant topic and to the Trilogy specification.

- [AQA GCSE Physics 8463](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/physics/gcse/physics-8463/specification/subject-content)
- [AQA Combined Science: Trilogy 8464, physics content](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/physics-subject-content)

Combined Science here means Trilogy, not Synergy. All counts below include both tiers. A Foundation filter removes Higher-only questions. There are 50 questions per separate Physics topic, including five numerical variants per calculation family; numbers decrease when course or tier restrictions are applied.

Examples of **shared Higher-only** content in the bank include efficiency improvements, repeated half-life calculations, momentum, area under velocity–time graphs, refraction explanations, radio-wave induction and the motor effect. The National Grid voltage/current explanation is shared; detailed transformer theory is Physics-only Higher. Physics-only does not imply Higher-only.

The bank covers calculations, explanations and selected practical/data skills, but does not cover every assessable bullet. The five numerical variants in each family practise the same relationship with different supplied values. Written questions have individual prompts and self-assessment points; self-assessment does not constitute an examiner-awarded grade.
