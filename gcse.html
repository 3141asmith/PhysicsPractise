Physics or Combined Science: what changes?

Comparison of AQA 8463 and the physics component of AQA 8464
FeatureGCSE Physics · 8463Combined Science: Trilogy · 8464
QualificationOne GCSE in PhysicsTwo GCSEs covering biology, chemistry and physics together
Physics examsTwo papers, each 1 hour 45 minutes and 100 marks; each worth 50% of PhysicsTwo physics papers within six science papers; each 1 hour 15 minutes and 70 marks, worth 16.7% of the full qualification
Paper 1Energy, electricity, particle model of matter and atomic structure
Paper 2Forces, waves, magnetism and electromagnetism, plus space physicsForces, waves, magnetism and electromagnetism; no space physics topic
Additional contentExtra material within shared topics, as well as space physics. Examples are highlighted on each topic card.Shared content only. A shared topic title does not mean every separate Physics subtopic is included.
TierBoth offer Foundation and Higher. Higher-only content is distinct from Physics-only content: some Higher material belongs to both courses.
Required practicalsIncludes extra investigations into thermal insulation and light reflection/refraction.Follow the Trilogy practical requirements; use its specification rather than the separate Physics practical numbering.

Paper 2 can also draw on earlier energy and electricity ideas. Practical skills, equations, units and interpreting data matter throughout both courses.
